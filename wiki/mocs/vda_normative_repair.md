# Normative VDA and repair lineage

## TL;DR

The 2026 normative VDA paper is a stationary signal-detection/ideal-observer model, not a transformer experiment. Its public-facing canonical correction is `Reconstruction/`, while `Rebuild/` remains the deepest technical and provenance record and `Critique/` preserves the adversarial audit.

## Plain explanation

The frozen source asks when value-directed attention—reallocating perceptual sensitivity toward a valuable location—adds reward beyond optimal decision-criterion adjustment. The repair process showed that several categorical claims needed distributional, graded, or conditional wording and added a decorrelation lever. Critique establishes what survived attack; Rebuild contains simulations, derivations, tests, and the claim-strength ceiling; Reconstruction assembles those repaired claims into the current readable manuscript.

## Canonical artifacts and roles

1. **Frozen source:** `Critique/source/main.pdf` — original framing and claims only.
2. **Adversarial audit:** `Critique/verdicts/`, `Critique/derivations/`, and `Critique/replications/` — current attack status and counterevidence.
3. **Deepest repair record:** `Rebuild/CLAIM_LEDGER.md`, `Rebuild/model/`, `Rebuild/sims/`, `Rebuild/derivations/`, and `Rebuild/manuscript/` — technical ceiling and provenance.
4. **Canonical corrected manuscript:** `Reconstruction/manuscript/main.tex`, `Reconstruction/manuscript/main.pdf`, and `Reconstruction/TRACE.md` — public-facing synthesis at ledger strength.

## Reading order

1. `Reconstruction/manuscript/main.pdf` for the current normative argument.
2. `Reconstruction/TRACE.md` for claim-to-artifact provenance.
3. `Rebuild/CLAIM_LEDGER.md` and named simulations/derivations for technical depth.
4. Current files under `Critique/verdicts/` when checking whether a claim survived attack.
5. `Critique/source/main.pdf` only when recovering the original paper's wording or framing.

## Evidence boundary

Neural checkpoints and behavioral batteries under [[rvit_plus]] can operationalize criterion, sensitivity, representation, or causal-routing measurements. They do not become normative proof merely because both projects use “VDA.” Conversely, the ideal-observer model does not establish what a trained recurrent network represents or causally uses without direct empirical measurement.
