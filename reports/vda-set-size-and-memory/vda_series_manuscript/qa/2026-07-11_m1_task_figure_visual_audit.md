# M1 task-figure visual audit — 2026-07-11

## Scope

This audit covers the fourteen deterministic M1 task figures generated by `RViT_plus_paper_jepa_grid9/vda_series/task_figures.py`:

- comparator: `validity4`;
- historical: `vda1`, `vda2`, `vda4`, `vda9`, `vda16`, and `vda_excl`;
- controlled: `vda_fixed1`, `vda_fixed2`, `vda_fixed4`, `vda_fixed9`, and `vda_fixed16`;
- zero-shot probe variants: `vda_probe_cued` and `vda_probe_uncued`.

Each environment has a vector PDF, 300-dpi PNG, and JSON metadata record under `reports/vda_series/figures/task/`. The shared contact sheet is `m1_task_contact_sheet.png`.

## Verification

- Focused tests: 25 passed in `tests/test_vda_series_task_figures.py`.
- Full `RViT_plus_paper_jepa_grid9` suite after the expanded implementation: 142 passed.
- All fourteen environments emitted PDF, PNG, and JSON outputs.
- Timeline frames were checked for seven-frame structure, blank/cue/delay timing, active-item geometry, and visible change frames.
- Realized target distributions were checked against preserved historical and prospective controlled sampling semantics.

## Visual inspection

The fourteen-figure contact sheet was inspected as a whole. Full-resolution inspection also covered the densest 4×4 condition (`vda_fixed16`) and the geometrically distinct exclusion condition (`vda_excl`).

No panel-edge clipping, title clipping, legend collision, blank failed output, or internal figure overlap was observed. The seven timeline frames remain distinct across 1×2, 2×2, 3×3, and 4×4 geometries. The orange value disk and white validity arc remain distinguishable from the grayscale Gabor arrays without relying on a multi-hue categorical legend. The neutral-value `validity4` cue, all four source validity configurations where applicable, and the cued-versus-uncued delay probes are visually distinct. The probability maps use perceptually ordered `viridis` colors and print numeric percentages in every cell.

Earlier renders exposed correctable issues: dark heatmap cells used black labels, the exclusion figure did not explicitly identify its distractor-present versus target-alone branches, and a hard-coded cue-gallery heading could collide at contact-sheet scale. The final render uses white text on dark probability cells, integer row/column ticks, explicit exclusion-branch labels, a bounded inapplicability note for the exclusion cue family, and no floating gallery heading. These corrections were re-rendered for all fourteen environments.

## Scientific boundary

M1 is a task-derived schematic. It documents task geometry, active-item count, timing, and target-sampling semantics. It contains no learned-model measurement and cannot establish performance, convergence, a capacity limit, or neural equivalence. Singleton panels display their validity degeneracy explicitly. Historical `vda16` receives a task schematic only; this does not turn its partial checkpoint lineage into a completed result.

## Verdict

PASS for standalone M1 figure generation and visual quality. Coverage remains globally partial until the figures are placed in each environment manuscript and the other twenty-one MAH source-object mappings are completed or explicitly marked undefined/blocked.
