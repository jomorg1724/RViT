\paragraph{Figure inventory.} The analyses of Section~\ref{sec:detection} are rendered
from the frozen analysis outputs of the trained model: psychometric and reaction-time
curves, per-condition attention super-figures and the $\alpha_c$ time-course, the
signal-detection $c(\alpha_c)$ and $d'(\alpha_c)$ curves under the graded attention
clamp, the distributional-critic uncertainty panels, the temporal-decoding curves, and
the microstimulation false-detection curves. The value-directed results
(Section~\ref{sec:value}) and the preliminary Luo--Maunsell comparison
(Section~\ref{sec:luomaunsell}) are rendered from their respective analysis harnesses.
Figure sources are noted inline in each section as \texttt{\% FIGURE:} comments.

\paragraph{Current battery status.} The VDA1/2/4/9 affine $d_{\mathrm{mem}}=128$
battery is reported in Section~\ref{sec:vda-battery}; it is a completed analysis in
this manuscript rather than a promised future appendix.
Each point uses one canonical checkpoint with a 20{,}000-row logged phase, and VDA2
uses the \texttt{\_2x2} checkpoint. VDA16 remains incomplete at checkpoint 599 and is
not a quantitative endpoint. Preserved $d_{\mathrm{mem}}=256$ checkpoints exist, but
the matched width battery is pending (Section~\ref{sec:capacity}). The delay-probe
environment is implemented, but no delay-probe or causal delay-clamp result is reported
(Section~\ref{sec:rehearsal}).

\paragraph{Invalid legacy artifacts.} The 2026-07-07 NPZ archives are retained for
provenance rather than overwritten. The VDA2/VDA9 change-location fields in
\path{vda_sweep/figs/decode.npz} are invalid because the legacy collector produced
degenerate labels. The high-token VDA9 \texttt{crossattn1} clamp fields in
\path{vda_sweep/figs/sdt.npz} are also invalid for cross-routing comparison because
the legacy indexer failed to bias the paired memory key. Neither field supports a
claim in this affine-only manuscript; the evidence ledger records the field-level
status without altering the archives. Corrected artifacts are pending in a versioned
derived path.
